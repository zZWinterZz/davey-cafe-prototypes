Our Story

Named After the Lamp
That Lit the Way

Sir Humphry Davy's safety lamp guided Yorkshire miners since 1815 — its warm amber glow and protective mesh a symbol of care and reliability. That's what we bring to Carlton Road every day.

"Honest food, proper portions, and a friendly face. The way it should be."

The Heritage
