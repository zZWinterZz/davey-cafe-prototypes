The Lamp

The Davy lamp was invented by Sir Humphry Davy in 1815 — its mesh screen stopped mine gas igniting while casting a warm amber glow. We carry that spirit of safety and warmth into every dish.

The Community

Carlton Road, Barnsley has been working-class heart since the colliery days. We're proud to serve the same community — unpretentious, generous, and always a proper portion.

The Kitchen

Everything's cooked fresh to order. No heat lamps, no shortcuts. If it takes a few extra minutes, it's because your breakfast is being done right.
