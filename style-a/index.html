Our Story

Lit by Tradition,
Driven by Flavour

Named after Sir Humphry Davy's miner's safety lamp — the light that guided Barnsley's pit workers through the dark — The Davy Lamp Café was founded in 2019 to bring the same warmth and reliability to the Carlton Road community.

Like the pit villages that built South Yorkshire's proud identity, we believe in honest, hearty food made with fresh, locally sourced ingredients. Every dish we serve is made fresh each morning.

Whether you're a miner's grandchild or a newcomer to Barnsley, you'll always find a warm welcome and a proper brew.
